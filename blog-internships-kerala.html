Top Biotech Internships in Kerala 2026 — Complete Guide for BSc Students

📅 May 2025 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ Bio360 Team 🏷 Internships · Kerala · BSc
If you're a BSc Life Science, Biotechnology, Microbiology or Biochemistry student in Kerala looking for a research internship — this is the most complete list you'll find. We've covered eligibility, deadlines, how to apply and insider tips for each institution.

Why Internships Matter for BSc Students in Kerala

A research internship during your BSc degree does three things no textbook can: it gives you real lab experience, it strengthens your postgraduate entrance exam applications (CSIR NET, GATE BT, DBT-JRF), and it builds connections with researchers who can write recommendation letters for your MSc or PhD applications abroad.

The best part? Kerala has some of India's premier research institutions concentrated in Thiruvananthapuram — making it one of the best states for BSc students to access world-class labs.

Top Institutions Offering Internships in Kerala

RGCB — Rajiv Gandhi Centre for Biotechnology
📍 Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
Most Prestigious
RGCB is Kerala's premier biotechnology research institute under the Department of Biotechnology, Govt of India. Research areas include cancer biology, infectious diseases, molecular medicine, and plant biology. Getting an internship here significantly strengthens any MSc or PhD application.
Eligibility: BSc 2nd/3rd year, CGPA 7.5+
Duration: 4–8 weeks (summer)
Apply: Email individual PI directly
Stipend: Nominal (₹500–2000/month)
NIIST — National Institute for Interdisciplinary Science & Technology
📍 Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
CSIR Institute
NIIST is a CSIR laboratory focusing on green chemistry, materials science, and biosystems engineering. Offers summer internships through the CSIR-JIGYASA and direct application routes. Good for students interested in biotechnology applications and chemical biology.
Eligibility: BSc/MSc students
Duration: 4–6 weeks
Apply: CSIR portal or direct email to labs
Stipend: ₹1,500–3,000/month
IISER TVM — Indian Institute of Science Education and Research
📍 Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
Highly Competitive
IISER TVM runs a structured Summer Student Research Programme (SSRP) every year. Departments include Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. Excellent for students targeting PhD programs — IISER internship experience is recognized by international institutions.
Eligibility: BSc 2nd year+, CGPA 8.0+
Duration: 6–8 weeks (May–July)
Apply: iisertvm.ac.in/SSRP portal
Stipend: ₹5,000–8,000/month
Kerala Institute of Medical Sciences (KIMS) Research
📍 Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
Clinical Research
For students interested in clinical research, microbiology diagnostics, and medical biotechnology. KIMS offers project-based internships in their research division — ideal for those targeting medical lab technology or pharmaceutical careers.
Eligibility: BSc Microbiology, Biotechnology
Duration: 4–12 weeks
Apply: Direct email to research dept
JNTBGRI — Jawaharlal Nehru Tropical Botanic Garden
📍 Palode, Thiruvananthapuram
Plant Science
Excellent for students specializing in botany, plant biotechnology, and conservation biology. JNTBGRI maintains one of India's largest tropical plant collections and does active research in ethnobotany and plant genomics.
Eligibility: BSc Botany, Biotechnology
Duration: 4–8 weeks
Apply: jntbgri.res.in or direct email

How to Write a Cold Email That Gets a Response

Most students email the institution's general inbox and never hear back. The correct approach is to find the email of a specific Principal Investigator (PI) whose research matches your interest, and email them directly.

📧 Cold Email Template (Copy This)

Subject: Summer Internship Request — BSc Biotechnology, [Your University]

Dear Dr. [Name],

I am [Your Name], a [Semester] BSc [Subject] student at [College], affiliated with [University] (CGPA: [X.X]). I am writing to express my interest in a summer research internship in your laboratory, specifically in the area of [specific topic from their recent paper].

I have completed coursework in [relevant subjects] and have hands-on experience with [techniques]. I am particularly drawn to your work on [specific research area] and believe this internship would significantly advance my research skills.

I have attached my CV and am available from [date] to [date]. I would be grateful for any opportunity to contribute to your lab.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]

Pan-India Opportunities Worth Applying From Kerala

  • NCBS Bangalore — National Centre for Biological Sciences summer programme (highly competitive, excellent for PhD prep)
  • IISc Bangalore — Summer Research Fellowship Programme, apply through IAS/IISc/INSA jointly
  • JNCASR Bangalore — Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research internship programme
  • CCMB Hyderabad — Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology for genetics and genomics students
  • NIPGR Delhi — National Institute of Plant Genome Research for plant molecular biology

What to Include in Your CV for Internship Applications

  • CGPA prominently at the top (if above 7.5)
  • Lab techniques you know — even from practical classes (PCR, gel electrophoresis, titration, microscopy)
  • Any projects, seminars or poster presentations done in college
  • Courses taken relevant to the lab's research area
  • Honest statement of availability and travel readiness

Application Timeline for 2026 Summer Internships

  • October–December 2025 — Research institutes, read recent papers from labs you're interested in
  • January 2026 — Start sending cold emails to RGCB, NIIST, IISER PIs
  • February 2026 — Apply to IISER SSRP portal when it opens
  • March 2026 — Apply for IAS/IISc/INSA Summer Research Fellowship
  • May–July 2026 — Internship period for most institutes
