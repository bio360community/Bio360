Academic CV

CV Tips for Research &
Scholarship Applications

📋
Structure Order
Research CVs differ from industry CVs. Always lead with Education → Research Experience → Publications → Conferences → Skills.
Education (GPA, year)
Research Experience
Publications/Posters
Skills & Certifications
🔬
Research Experience
Each role needs 3 bullet points: what you did → technique/tool used → outcome or result. Quantify where possible.
• Conducted PCR analysis on 45 bacterial isolates using gel electrophoresis to confirm gene presence
📊
CGPA Presentation
Show CGPA out of 10 AND convert to 4.0 scale for international applications. Include class rank if in top 20%.
CGPA: 8.4/10 (3.5/4.0)
Class Rank: 3/60 (Top 5%)
🏆
Awards & Recognition
Include university ranks, merit scholarships, quiz wins, CSIR NET qualification. These signal academic performance to global reviewers.
CSIR NET LS Qualified (June 2024)
University Gold Medalist 2023
💻
Technical Skills
Group by category. Don't just list — indicate proficiency level. For bioinformatics roles, list specific tools.
Bioinformatics: BLAST, MEGA11, Biopython
Lab: PCR, gel electrophoresis, cell culture
Programming: Python (intermediate), R (basic)
📏
Length & Format
Research/scholarship CVs: 2 pages maximum for BSc, 3 for MSc. Use consistent fonts. PDF only. No photos for European applications.
Font: Times New Roman 11pt or Calibri 11pt
Margins: 2.5cm all sides
Format: PDF · 2 pages max

LOR Guidance

How to Get Strong
Recommendation Letters

Most students ask at the wrong time, in the wrong way. Here's the complete timeline and approach.

NOW — BUILD RELATIONSHIP
Do excellent work in lab/class
Professors write strong LORs for students they know well. Join a lab, be consistent, ask intelligent questions. A professor who knows your work writes 10× better letters than one who just knows your grade.
3 MONTHS BEFORE DEADLINE
Send a formal LOR request email
Email subject: "LOR Request for [Scholarship] — [Your Name]". Attach your CV, scholarship details, SOP draft, and a bullet list of your contributions to their lab/class. Give them everything they need.
6 WEEKS BEFORE
Send a reminder with materials
Send a polite reminder with the exact deadline, submission link/instructions, and offer to meet to discuss any questions. Never assume they've started.
2 WEEKS BEFORE
Final confirmation
Confirm receipt of LOR or check status. Thank them in advance. After submission, always send a personal thank-you email regardless of outcome.
LOR Request Email Template →

Subject: Letter of Recommendation Request — [Scholarship Name] · Dear Dr. [Name], I am applying for the [Scholarship] and would be honoured if you could write a letter of recommendation supporting my application. I have attached my CV, a draft SOP, and a summary of my contributions to [lab/project]. The deadline is [date] and the submission link is [URL]. I would be happy to discuss further at your convenience.
